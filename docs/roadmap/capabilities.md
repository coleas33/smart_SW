# Capabilities, evidence and effort

What is built, what is tested offline, and what an engineer has seen and triaged at the pilot
workstation, package by package; then the pilot evidence ledger, the effort ledger and the task
counts behind them. Written by roadmap run 1 on 2026-10-31 at `main` `a1ff6af6`; the roadmap
itself is [`README.md`](README.md) and its holes are in [`holes.md`](holes.md).

**Built is not validated.** A row says "seen at the seat" only where a dated seat record shows an
engineer using it on a real design: a sitting record in [`docs/history/`](../history/README.md),
a ticked seat task with its dated note, or the 2026-09-26 sitting as
[`specs/013-engineer-first-review/spec.md`](../../specs/013-engineer-first-review/spec.md) tells it.
No ticked seat task in any package is such a record (see the appendix), so every seat line below comes
from the dated sitting records. The results sheet
[`docs/test-plan-2026-09-27/workstation-results.md`](../test-plan-2026-09-27/workstation-results.md)
is a blank template, not a record.

The cells were written by five read-only scouts (one per three packages) and are kept as they
wrote them, with their citations; an inference is labelled as one. The done and open counts of
each package are in [`specs/README.md`](../../specs/README.md); section 6 lists where they lag
the task lists.
